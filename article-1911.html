15 October 1911

Jewellery Vanishes During Private Auction

LONDON, 15 October 1911

A rare jewel belonging to Lady Eleanor Ashcombe disappeared during a private auction held in London on the evening of 14 October.

The jewel, known among collectors as the Black Dahlia, was described as an exceptionally valuable piece consisting of a central crystal surrounded by five diamonds.

Witnesses reported confusion during the closing moments of the auction, after which the jewel could no longer be located.

No thief has been identified, and no recovery has been reported.

The private auction was conducted by Blackwood & Vale, whose catalogue had been prepared by the firm's senior registrar, Arthur Vale.
